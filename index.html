Frequently Asked Questions

What programs and services does Yuvantara provide?

Yuvantara provides enterprise cybersecurity solutions, IT infrastructure implementation, 24×7 managed services, as well as hands-on industrial internship programs in Cybersecurity, AI/ML, Cloud DevOps, Python, and Robotics with verified certifications.

How do I apply for an internship at Yuvantara?

Click on the Apply for Internship button in the navigation or on any track card. Select your preferred domain, provide your academic details, stream, and college name. Our academic counselor will contact you within 24 hours.

How can students and employers verify a Yuvantara Certificate?

Go to the Resources dropdown in the top menu and select Verify Certificate. Enter the unique Certificate ID (e.g. YUV-2026-CS101) to instantly view the authenticated candidate details, completion grade, and issuance date.

Where is Yuvantara located?

Our primary office is at Lekha Nagar, Saguna More, Patna, Bihar. We serve clients and students across India and globally.

Are Yuvantara internships recognized and accredited?

Yes. Yuvantara is an ISO 9001:2015 certified and MSME-registered organisation. Our certificates are recognized by academic institutions and corporate recruiters across the country.
