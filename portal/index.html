Yuvantara Corporate & Internship Policies (2026 Edition)

Mandatory corporate governance, data confidentiality, engineering standards, and certification criteria governing all enrolled candidates.

Official HR Handbook 2026
📋 Click any policy or the + icon to expand and read full policy clauses.

All candidates accepted into the Yuvantara Industrial Program represent the organization and must maintain the highest standards of professional conduct, technical diligence, and corporate ethics throughout the internship duration.

Core Rule: Unprofessional behavior, harassment, discrimination based on gender, caste, race, or religion, or deliberate disruption of peer collaboration will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Communication Protocols: Interns must use authorized corporate channels (designated Slack workspaces, Microsoft Teams, and official email) for all program-related interactions. Professional tone and respectful discourse are mandatory.
  • Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH): Yuvantara maintains zero tolerance for any form of harassment under the PoSH Act 2013. Any inappropriate behavior in virtual meeting rooms or chat channels results in immediate expulsion.
  • Camera & Meeting Etiquette: Video presence is required during one-on-one mentor evaluations, milestone defenses, and client engagement simulations with formal or business-casual attire.
  • Punctuality & Commitments: Interns must join technical scrums on time and fulfill deliverable commitments made to their assigned technical leads.

Yuvantara Venture operates in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the Information Technology (IT) Act 2000 (with amendments). All interns are bound by the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) executed upon program onboarding.

Legal Warning: Any unauthorized exfiltration, screenshotting, downloading, or public transmission of proprietary company datasets, candidate PII, or security threat intelligence constitutes a punishable cyber offense under Section 43A and 72A of the IT Act.
  • Definition of Confidential Data: Includes internal vulnerability assessment data, client test targets, proprietary AI algorithms, student PII (DOB, contact numbers, email hashes), and internal billing workflows.
  • Device & Storage Boundaries: Production or staging datasets must never be downloaded to unencrypted personal thumb drives, external hard drives, or unapproved third-party cloud storage (e.g., personal Google Drive, Mega, Dropbox).
  • Right to Privacy & Data Erasure: Candidate records collected during registration are safeguarded under 256-bit encryption. Candidates may exercise their data correction or review rights by writing to the Data Protection Officer.
  • Post-Internship Obligations: The confidentiality covenants under this agreement remain legally binding for a period of three (3) years post completion or termination of the internship.

Engineering excellence and intellectual integrity are foundational to Yuvantara's industrial programs. All project artifacts, architectural blueprints, scripts, and software artifacts must satisfy rigorous industry standards.

IP Assignment: All intellectual property rights, trademarks, patents, software codebases, and architectures developed in whole or in part during the internship assignment remain the exclusive intellectual property of Yuvantara Venture.
  • Repository Governance: All commits must be made to company-allocated Git repositories under established feature branch workflows (`feat/`, `fix/`, `docs/`) with descriptive commit messages complying with Conventional Commits.
  • Anti-Plagiarism & Original Work: Submitting copied, scraped, or plagiarized code from open-source repositories without proper permissible attribution (MIT/Apache 2.0) is grounds for immediate project disqualification. Automated SonarQube and MOSS checks are executed on all final pull requests.
  • License Hygiene: Copyleft licenses (such as GPLv3, AGPL) must NOT be introduced into proprietary frameworks without explicit prior written consent from the Chief Technology Officer.
  • Deliverable Documentation: Every Capstone project deliverable must include comprehensive README documentation, architectural diagrams, API schema documentation, and automated unit/integration test suites.

Yuvantara provisions high-performance cloud virtual private clouds (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure), Dockerized microservices, and dedicated cybersecurity simulation ranges for intern training and Capstone implementations.

Prohibited Cyber Activities: Engaging in unauthorized network port scanning, brute-forcing, cryptocurrency mining, denial-of-service (DDoS) simulations, or targeting external third-party IP addresses will trigger instant credential revocation and legal escalation.
  • Authorized Toolchains: Interns must only use sanctioned IDEs (VS Code, IntelliJ, PyCharm), official compilers, and containerized Docker images provided in the onboarding manifest.
  • Secrets Management: Never commit API keys, database credentials, AWS access keys, or JWT private keys to git repositories. All secrets must be loaded via local environment variables (`.env`) listed in `.gitignore`.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): All developer portal accounts, Git providers, and cloud consoles must have MFA enforced via Authenticator apps (Google Authenticator / YubiKey).
  • Resource Clean-Up & Cost Control: Cloud instances (EC2, GCE, Kubernetes pods) must be spun down at the end of each lab exercise. Leaving compute instances idle needlessly wastes infrastructure quota and may lead to lab suspension.

Industrial programs require continuous, sustained involvement. Irregular attendance hampers technical skill acquisition and compromises project milestone delivery.

Attendance Threshold: A verified minimum attendance of 85% across all live technical lectures, hands-on labs, and sprint syncs is strictly required to qualify for certificate issuance.
  • Weekly Progress Reports (WPR): Every intern must submit their weekly log through the Student Portal by every Friday, 6:00 PM IST, detailing completed milestones, blockers encountered, and planned deliverables for the coming week.
  • Sprint Review Syncs: Weekly or bi-weekly 1-on-1 sprint reviews with assigned industry mentors and defense leads are compulsory. Unpreparedness or repeated no-shows will lower evaluation grades.
  • Planned Academic / Medical Leaves: Requests for leave due to semester examinations or medical emergencies must be emailed at least 48 hours in advance to yuvantara@yuvantaraventure.com with supporting college documentation.
  • Automatic Suspension: Unexcused absence exceeding five (5) consecutive working days without formal communication results in automatic program suspension and notification to the academic coordinator.

Evaluation at Yuvantara is objective, performance-driven, and designed to mirror tier-1 technology company engineering reviews.

Certification Prerequisite: To become eligible for the official industrial credential, candidate progress must reach 100% with all 5 core modules signed off and a passing grade on the Capstone Defense.
  • Evaluation Breakdown:
    • Hands-on Lab Assignments & Module Quizzes: 30%
    • Weekly Attendance, Code Review & Mentor Ratings: 20%
    • Final Capstone Engineering Defense & Code Quality: 50%
  • Grading Scale:
    • Grade A+ (Distinction ≥ 90%): Flawless implementation, exemplary architecture, robust security posture, exceptional defense.
    • Grade A (Merit 75%–89%): Thorough implementation satisfying all functional specifications with clean code and tests.
    • Grade B (Pass 60%–74%): Satisfactory implementation meeting baseline functional criteria.
  • Remedial Grace Window: Candidates scoring below 60% are granted a single 14-day grace window to address mentor code-review feedback and resubmit their Capstone deliverable for re-evaluation.

Upon successfully reaching 100% course milestone completion, an official industrial certificate of completion is instantly auto-generated by the academic engine, complete with an anti-tamper QR code and unique Certificate ID.

Revocation Policy: Yuvantara Venture reserves the absolute and irrevocable right to revoke any certificate previously issued if post-issuance audits discover academic dishonesty, identity fabrication, plagiarism, or breach of confidentiality.
  • Immediate Generation: No manual waiting period or administrative delay is required once 100% curriculum completion is verified. The candidate can immediately preview, download, and print their official document.
  • Live National Registry Verification: Every certificate bears a high-density, cryptographically structured QR code that links directly to the real-time public verification portal at yuvantaraventure.com/verify-certificate/.
  • Permanent Revocation Registry: If a certificate is revoked, its status is permanently updated to Revoked on the public verification portal, displaying the revocation date and administrative reference, and an official notification is dispatched to the candidate's affiliated university.

Yuvantara is committed to providing a transparent, fair, and supportive academic and industrial training environment. A structured grievance redressal mechanism is in place to address candidate concerns promptly and impartially.

Grievance Escalation Contact: Candidates may submit confidential academic, evaluation, or personal welfare grievances directly to the Grievance Nodal Officer at yuvantara@yuvantaraventure.com.
  • Service Level Agreement (SLA): Every grievance receipt is formally acknowledged within 12 business hours, and formal hearing or resolution is concluded within 48 business hours.
  • Grade Re-Check Requests: Candidates contesting their Capstone score or module evaluation may submit a formal re-evaluation request within seven (7) days of score announcement.
  • Whistleblower & Retaliation Protection: Any candidate reporting security policy violations, data misuse, or harassment is granted strict anonymity and complete immunity from academic or administrative retaliation.
